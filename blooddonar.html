Receiving a blood transfusion is a safe and common procedure. We're here to provide clear information and compassionate care to give you peace of mind.

Ready for Emergencies

Your Transfusion Journey

A simple, safe, and monitored process from start to finish.

1

Doctor's Decision & Consent

Your doctor will explain why a transfusion is needed and answer all your questions before you provide consent.

2

Blood Sample & Matching

A nurse takes a small blood sample to determine your blood type and find a perfect match in our blood bank.

3

The Transfusion

An IV is started, and the matched blood is slowly and painlessly administered over a few hours.

4

Constant Monitoring

Your nurse will check on you frequently to ensure you are comfortable and feeling well throughout the process.
