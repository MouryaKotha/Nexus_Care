Privacy-First

Your privacy matters

Nexus Care is designed using data-minimization principles. We only collect what is strictly necessary to provide you with care.

What Nexus Care collects

  • Account Information

    Name, email address, and secure authentication information.

  • AI Mentor Data

    Conversation text required for history, and numeric speech metrics (e.g. speaking duration) if voice is used.

  • Health Vault

    Medical records and files you explicitly upload.

What we do NOT collect

Raw audio is never stored in our databases or servers.

  • Raw microphone recordings
  • Audio files or blobs
  • Temporary voice recordings

When using voice features, speech is processed locally in your browser. Only the resulting text and numeric metrics are transmitted.

Privacy Settings

  • Analytics & Usage

    Help us improve by sharing anonymous usage data.

  • Speech Metrics

    Allow analysis of vocal pause length and duration for better AI mentoring.

Data Retention

  • AI Mentor

    Stored while required for your conversation history, unless you delete it.

  • Health Vault

    Stored until you delete the record or account.

  • Account

    Stored while your account remains active.

Manage Your Data

AI Mentor Data

Delete your stored conversation history and numeric speech metrics.

Health Vault Data

Delete all your uploaded medical records and associated metadata.

Community Data

Remove your activity from the community hub.

All Personal Data

Permanently delete all application data associated with your account.
